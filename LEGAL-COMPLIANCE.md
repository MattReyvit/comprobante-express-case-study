# Legal compliance mapping
| Requirement | Peru (applied) | Spain / EU (equivalent) |
|---|---|---|
| Personal data | Law 29733, D.S. 016-2024-JUS | GDPR, LOPDGDD |
| Data subject rights | ARCO rights | Access, rectification, erasure, portability |
| E-invoicing | SUNAT electronic receipts | Veri*factu, Crea y Crece law |
| Consumer protection | Law 29571, complaints book | Consumer law, complaint forms |
| Record keeping | Tax retention periods | Tax retention periods, immutable logs |

## Practices applied
- Invoices are never edited after submission; corrections via credit notes.
- Every submission is logged with status and timestamp.
- Access to fiscal data restricted per business account.
